# 14 Degree of skill

Learners at Vantage level may be expected to communicate not only more effectively but also more efficiently and with greater ease, possessing a degree of communicative skill that enables them to cope with the unforeseen without being unduly hindered by gaps in their ability.

## Accuracy
Accuracy involves: pronunciation (including rhythm and intonation); spelling; and lexical, idiomatic and grammatical correctness.
* **Pronunciation**: Pronunciation will in no way interfere with being easily understood by native speakers, as well as non-native speakers at or above Threshold level. Native speakers with little or no experience dealing with foreigners should have no difficulty understanding them, and communication should not be laborious.
* **Spelling**: Mistakes will be either absent or rare. Learners will have acquired sound spelling habits and can solve doubts by consulting a dictionary or choosing alternative structures.
* **Lexical, Idiomatic, and Grammatical Correctness**: High importance is attached to formal correctness. Learners will be familiar with and skilled in using more commonly used grammatical structures, rarely producing ungrammatical forms. Gaps in lexicon and idiom will be managed smoothly via repair strategies so that learners satisfy their communication needs without feeling hindered by a sense of inadequacy.

## Appropriacy
Appropriacy involves: sociolinguistic appropriacy; sociocultural appropriacy; and social appropriacy.
* **Sociolinguistic & Sociocultural**: Involves role adequacy, register, the realization of intentions, and the adequacy of use of the sociocultural reference frame. Learners will have a high degree of awareness and skill to ensure adequate participation in events involving native speakers, generally adopting 'unmarked' options that avoid the extremes of formality and familiarity.
* **Social Appropriacy**: Relates to the interpersonal handling of contacts, which depends heavily on individual personality features and is less suitable as a pure testing criterion.

## Fluency
Fluency involves: ease of access to and retrieval from memory; command of discourse strategies; and rate of delivery and processing.
* Unnaturally long pauses in production and extreme delays in interpretation will be rare.
* Compensatory competence (repair and avoidance strategies) is sufficiently developed to maintain the continuous flow of communicative interaction despite gaps in linguistic ability.

## Summary
At Vantage level, the learners will have achieved a degree of skill that gives them the confidence that they can cope adequately with the requirements of the large majority of communicative situations, foreseen or unforeseen, achieving a successful outcome without being unduly hindered by the need to use the foreign language.
