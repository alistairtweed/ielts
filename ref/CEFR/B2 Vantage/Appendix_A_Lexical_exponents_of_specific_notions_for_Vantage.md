# Appendix A: Lexical exponents of specific notions for Vantage

This appendix provides a systematic listing of lexical exponents of specific notions across the primary themes of the Threshold and Vantage levels. It is an open-ended listing meant to stimulate curriculum choices and support the individualized lexical expansion required for Vantage-level task performance.
