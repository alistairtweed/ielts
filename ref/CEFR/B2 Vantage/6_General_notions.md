# 6 General notions

## Introduction
The list of general notions is derived from a consideration of what, in general, people deal with by means of language, irrespective of the specific communication situation. They are classified into eight categories below.

## 1. Existential
* **1.1 Existence, non-existence**: *There is + NP*, *There's no + NP*, *There isn't any + NP*. Verbs: *to exist, to become, to make, to create, to design, to produce; to destroy; to appear, to disappear*.
* **1.2 Presence, absence**: *here, not here, there, not there, away; present, absent; presence, absence*.
* **1.3 Availability, non-availability**: *to have (got)*, *There is (no) + NP*, *available, not available; ready*.
* **1.4 Occurrence, non-occurrence**: *to happen, to occur, to take place; event, occurrence*.
* **1.5 Discovery, non-discovery**: *to find, to discover; to avoid, to miss; discovery*.

## 2. Spatial
* **2.1 Location**: Nouns: *location, place, position, site, situation*. Adverbs: *here, there, here and there, everywhere, somewhere, nowhere, (not) anywhere, where?, wherever; inside, outside; in the east/north/south/west*. Verbs: *to be, to be situated, to lie*. Determiners: *this, that, these, those*.
* **2.2 Relative position**: Prepositions: *above, against, among, at, at the end of, at the back of, at the side of, before, behind, below, beside, between, in front of, inside, in the centre of, next to, on, opposite, outside, over, round, under, with*. Clauses: *where + sub-clause*. Adverbs/phrases: *above, below, at the end, at the back, at the side, behind, in the background, in front, inside, in the middle, in the centre, opposite, outside*. Verbs: *to cover, to overlap*.
* **2.3 Distance**: Nouns/Adjectives: *distance, distant, far (away) (from), remote, near, in the neighbourhood (of), close (to), ... away*.
* **2.4 Motion**: Nouns: *motion, movement*. Verbs: *to arrive, to come, to come along, to come + to NP, to enter, to drop, to fall, to flow, to get up, to go, to hurry, to leave, to lie down, to move, to pass, to quit, to remove, to roll, to run, to stand still, to start, to step, to stop, to walk*.
* **2.5 Direction**: Nouns: *direction, destination, direct*. Adverbs: *away, back, backward(s), (anti-)clockwise, down, downward(s), forward(s), in, inward(s), out, outward(s), (to the) left/right, straight on, up, upward(s), east, north, south, west*. Prepositions: *across, along, down, for, from, into, off, past, through, to, towards, up, away from*. Verbs: *to bring, to carry, to follow, to pull, to push, to put, to send, to take, to take away, to turn*.
* **2.6 Origin**: Prepositions/Adverbs: *from, out of*. Nouns: *origin, root(s)*.
* **2.7 Arrangement**: *after, before, between, among, first, last; arrangement, pattern, set*.
* **2.8 Dimension**:
  * **2.8.1 Size**: Adjectives: *big, deep, enormous, great, high, huge, large, little, long, low, narrow, shallow, short, small, tall, thick, thin, tiny, wide*. Verbs: *to become/get bigger/smaller, to increase, to decrease, to grow, to shrink*. Nouns: *depth, height, length, size, thickness, width*.
  * **2.8.2 Length**: *length; centimetre, foot, inch, kilometre, metre, mile, millimetre, yard; long, short*.
  * **2.8.3 Pressure**: *heavy, light, high, low pressure; to press*.
  * **2.8.4 Weight**: *to weigh; load, weight; gram(me)s, kilo, lbs., oz., ton(ne); light, heavy*.
  * **2.8.5 Volume**: *content; gallon, litre, pint; to contain*.
  * **2.8.6 Space**: *big, small; room, space*.
  * **2.8.7 Temperature**: *temperature, degree, zero, heat, cold; chilly, cold, cool, hot, lukewarm, mild, warm; to boil, to burn, to freeze, to heat, to get cold/hot/warm*.

## 3. Temporal
* **3.1 Points of time**: *clock times, half past, a.m./p.m., noon, midnight, 24-hour clock clock (e.g., 1500, 1518), at...*
* **3.2 Division of time**: *moment, period, second, minute, quarter of an hour, half hour, hour, day, week, fortnight, month, year, century; seasons (autumn, spring, summer, winter); afternoon, evening, morning, night, weekend, holiday(s)*. Names of days and months.
* **3.3 Indications of time**: *time, now, then, when?, whenever, soon, ago, today, tomorrow, yesterday, the day after tomorrow, the day before yesterday, this morning, tonight, last/next week, dates (1 June), Christmas, Easter*. Prepositions: *at, by, in, on*.
* **3.4 Duration**: Prepositions: *during, for, since, throughout, till, until, not... till*. Verbs: *to continue, to go on, to last, to take*. Adjectives: *long, short, quick*. Adverbs: *always, for good*.
* **3.5 Earliness**: *early, early train*.
* **3.6 Lateness**: *late, too late, late show*.
* **3.7 Anteriority**: *present perfect, past perfect, before + sub-clause, before, already, yet*.
* **3.8 Posteriority**: *after + NP/sub-clause, afterwards, later (on), later than...*
* **3.9 Sequence**: *first, then, next, finally, at last, later on, in the end, afterwards*.
* **3.10 Simultaneousness**: *when + sub-clause, while + sub-clause, as soon as + sub-clause, in the course of + NP, at the same time, in the meantime, meanwhile, instant(ly)*.
* **3.11 Future reference**: *NP + be going to..., NP + shall/will..., present continuous of verbs of motion, simple present (with future adverbials), in (e.g., in four weeks), next week, tonight, tomorrow, immediately, soon, ultimately, future, the future*.
* **3.12 Present reference**: *present continuous, simple present, present perfect, at present, now, today, still, this morning*.
* **3.13 Past reference**: *past continuous, simple past, yesterday, the day before yesterday, formerly, just, just now, recent(ly), lately, of late, last week, in the past, originally*.
* **3.14 Reference without time focus**: *simple present (e.g., Edinburgh is in Scotland.)*
* **3.15 Delay**: *later, delay, to be delayed*.
* **3.16 Speed**: *speed, fast, rapid(ly), quick(ly), slow(ly), miles/kilometres per hour*.
* **3.17 Frequency**: *always, (hardly) ever, frequent(ly), never, occasional(ly), (not) often, once, rarely, repeatedly, seldom, sometimes, twice, usually; daily, weekly, monthly, annual(ly), hourly; now and then; times a week, on weekdays, every hour*.
* **3.18 Continuity**: *present/past continuous, present perfect, to continue, to go on*.
* **3.19 Intermittence**: *not always, sometimes, occasionally, on and off*.
* **3.20 Permanence**: *always, for ever, for good, permanent(ly)*.
* **3.21 Temporariness**: *for + NP, not always, temporary, temporarily, present/past continuous*.
* **3.22 Repetitiousness**: *again, again and again; all the time, many times, several times, to repeat*.
* **3.23 Uniqueness**: *(only) once*.
* **3.24 Commencement**: *to begin, to start, to go, from... since..., at first, initial(ly), beginning, start*.
* **3.25 Cessation**: *end, finish, stop, till, until, to*.
* **3.26 Stability**: *to remain, to stay, to keep, to wait*.
* **3.27 Change, transition**: *to become, change, to get, to interrupt, to turn, to vary, suddenly*.

## 4. Quantitative
* **4.1 Number**: Singular/plural, cardinal numerals, ordinal numerals, fractions, minus, plus, negative, positive, number, to count, about, approximately, exactly, another.
* **4.2 Quantity**: All, a lot of, any, both, each, enough, (a) few, (a) little, less, least, many, more, most, how much, how many, much, no, several, some, various, double, twice, half, at least, extra, further, plenty (of), per cent, sum, total, whole, to add, to subtract, to multiply, to divide, partitive nouns (*a bottle/box/cup/glass/packet/piece of*).
* **4.3 Degree**: Comparative and superlative degrees, enough, too, very, a bit, a little, somewhat, a lot, much, almost, hardly, quite, rather, slightly, so, absolutely, completely, such, even.

## 5. Qualitative
* **5.1 Physical properties**:
  * **5.1.1 Shape**: *circular, crooked, curved, oval, rectangular, round, square, straight, circle, curve, form, oval, rectangle, shape, square*.
  * **5.1.2 Dimension**: See General Notions 2.8.
  * **5.1.3 Moisture, humidity**: *damp, dry, humid, moist, wet; to dry, to wet; humidity, moisture*.
  * **5.1.4 Visibility, sight**: *see, look (at), watch, look, glance, sight, darkness, blind, (in)visible, dark, light*.
  * **5.1.5 Audibility, hearing**: *hear, listen (to), noise, silence, sound, deaf, loud, noisy, silent, soft, quiet*.
  * **5.1.6 Taste**: *to taste (of), flavour, taste, bitter, salt(y), sour, sweet, bad, excellent, lovely, nice*.
  * **5.1.7 Smell**: *to smell (of), smell, odour, perfume, pleasant, unpleasant*.
  * **5.1.8 Texture**: *hard, rough, smooth, soft, strong, weak*.
  * **5.1.9 Colour**: *colour, blue, black, brown, green, grey, orange, pink, purple, red, white, yellow, light, bright, dull, dark*.
  * **5.1.10 Age**: *age, years old, X-year-old, adult, elderly, mature, middle-aged, old, young, baby, child, adolescent, teenager, childhood, adolescence, maturity, old age, generation, week/month/year, under/over age, come of age*.
  * **5.1.11 Physical condition**: *to break, to cut, to be hurt, to die, to fasten, to fix, to recover, to repair, to put right, to look well, death, health, life, sickness, alive, all right, better, dead, ill, well, in/out of order*.
  * **5.1.12 Accessibility**: *close, enter, get at, go in, open, reach, closed, open*.
  * **5.1.13 Cleanness**: *to clean, dust, polish, wash, clean, dirty, dusty, neat, soiled, cleanness, dirt, mess*.
  * **5.1.14 Material**: *cotton, fur, linen, leather, nylon, silk, wool(len), aluminium, brass, brick, cardboard, concrete, copper, glass, iron, metal, paper, plastic, polyester, steel, stone, wood(en), gold, platinum, silver, material, textile(s)*.
  * **5.1.15 Genuineness**: *false, genuine, imaginary, imitation, real*.
  * **5.1.16 Fullness**: *empty, full (of), to empty, to fill*.
* **5.2 Evaluative properties**:
  * **5.2.1 Value, price**: *How much is...?, to cost, price, value, cheap, (in)expensive, high, low, valuable*.
  * **5.2.2 Quality**: *quality, bad, worse, worst, excellent, fine, nice, perfect, poor, pure, outstanding, terrible, wonderful, to deteriorate, to improve, badly, excellently, nicely, perfectly, poorly, purely, outstandingly, terribly, well, wonderfully*.
  * **5.2.3 Rightness, wrongness**: *should/ought to, right, wrong*.
  * **5.2.4 Acceptability, non-acceptability**: *That's all right/fine/nice, just/not quite what I wanted, I don't like it, I cannot accept, I'm against, satisfactory, unsatisfactory*.
  * **5.2.5 Adequacy, inadequacy**: *all right, fine, just right, (not) enough, That will do*.
  * **5.2.6 Desirability, undesirability**: *to like*.
  * **5.2.7 Correctness, incorrectness**: *better, (in)correct, false, faulty, right, true, wrong, OK, to be right/wrong, to make better, to put right, to correct*.
  * **5.2.8 Successfulness, unsuccessfulness**: *to fail, to succeed, to try, failure, success, (un)successful*.
  * **5.2.9 Utility, inutility**: *can(not) use, helpful, practical, relevant, (not) useful, useless*.
  * **5.2.10 Capacity, incapacity**: *can(not), will/won't, be (un)able to, to enable*.
  * **5.2.11 Importance, unimportance**: *(not) important, unimportant, trivial*.
  * **5.2.12 Normality, abnormality**: *(ab)normal, strange, ordinary, extraordinary, (un)usual, unique*.
  * **5.2.13 Facility, difficulty**: *easy, difficult, hard, ease, difficulty*.
  * **5.2.14 Discovery, non-discovery**: *to discover, to find, to avoid, to miss*.
  * **5.2.15 Complexity, simplicity**: *complex, complicated, simple, complexity, simplicity*.
  * **5.2.16 Convenience, inconvenience**: *convenient, inconvenient, convenience, inconvenience*.
  * **5.2.17 Generality, specificity**: *general, global, individual, particular, special, typical, to consist of, whole, part, detail, feature*.

## 6. Mental
* **6.1 Reflection, intuition**: Verbs: *to believe, to be sure, to be certain, to change one's mind, to conclude, to consider, to hope, to ignore, to imagine, to know, to neglect, to overlook, to remember, to take into account, to think, to wonder*. Nouns: *belief, certainty, conclusion, hope, knowledge, opinion, thought, view*. Adverbs: *naturally, no doubt, of course, on second thoughts*.
* **6.2 Expression**: Verbs: *to answer, to apologise, to ask, to command, to communicate, to discuss, to forbid, to invite, to laugh, to recommend, to refer (to), to relate, to remark, to remind, to report, to respond, to request, to say, to speak, to suggest, to talk, to tell, to thank, to write*. Nouns: *answer, argument, communication, discussion, question, remark, report, statement, suggestion*.

## 7. Relational
* **7.1 Spatial relations**: See General Notions section 2.
* **7.2 Temporal relations**: See General Notions section 3.
* **7.3 Action/event relations**:
  * **7.3.1 Agency**: Agent as subject, agent in *by-adjunct*, agent in emphatic *It was I who...*
  * **7.3.2 Objective/Factitive**: Objective as object (*John opened the door*), objective as subject of passive (*The door was opened by John*), factitive as object (*She made a new dress*), factitive as subject of passive (*This cathedral was built in the thirteenth century*).
  * **7.3.3 Dative**: Dative as indirect object (*He gave me a book*), dative in *to-adjunct* (*He gave the ticket to my brother*), dative as subject of passive (*He was given a book*).
  * **7.3.4 Instrumental**: Instrumental in *with-adjunct* (*You can open the door with this key*).
  * **7.3.5 Benefactive**: Benefactive in *for-adjunct* (*I have bought this for my wife*).
  * **7.3.6 Causative**: *to have* (*Can I have my shirt washed, please?*), *to make* (*How can I make you understand?*).
  * **7.3.7 Place**: See General Notions section 2.
  * **7.3.8 Time**: See General Notions section 3.
  * **7.3.9 Manner, means**: *in this manner, in this way, like this, by means of, somehow, thus; badly, fast, hard, quickly, slowly, well, by + Ving, as*.
* **7.4 Contrastive relations**:
  * **7.4.1 Equality, inequality**: *(not) the same (thing) (as...), (dis)similar, different (from), else, other, another, difference, to compare*.
  * **7.4.2 Correspondence**: *like, unlike, equal (to), comparative degree + than, superlative degree, as... as, not so... as*.
* **7.5 Possessive relations**:
  * **7.5.1 Ownership, possession**: Possessive adjectives (*my, your*), possessive pronouns (*mine, yours*), genitive singular of personal nouns, *of-adjuncts*, *with-adjuncts, without-adjuncts*, *to belong to, to have (got), to get, to give, to keep, to obtain, to own, to possess, owner, own*.
* **7.6 Logical relations**:
  * **7.6.1 Conjunction**: *and, as well as, both... and, also, too, not... either, neither, nor, together, couple, pair, group*.
  * **7.6.2 Disjunction**: *or, however, either... or, separate(ly), one by one, on the other hand*.
  * **7.6.3 Inclusion, exclusion**: *with, without, except, (not) including, excluding, to exclude, to include, to omit*.
  * **7.6.4 Cause**: *cause, why?, because + sub-clause, as + sub-clause, due to, because of*.
  * **7.6.5 Effect**: *then, so, so that, the result is...*
  * **7.6.6 Reason**: *why?, because + sub-clause, since + sub-clause, the reason is...*
  * **7.6.7 Purpose**: *to..., in order to..., the purpose is...*
  * **7.6.8 Condition, dependence**: *if + sub-clause, unless + sub-clause, in case of, on condition that, to depend (on), dependent (on), independent (of), dependence, independence*.
  * **7.6.9 Focusing**: *about, on, as to, concerning, as regards, with regard to, only*.
  * **7.6.10 Concession**: *though, although, despite, in spite of, in fact*.

## 8. Deixis
* **8.1 Definite**:
  * **8.1.1 Non-anaphoric**: Personal pronouns, possessive adjectives, possessive pronouns as complement/subject, demonstrative adjectives and pronouns (*this, that, these, those, such*), independent relative pronoun (*what*), definite article (*the*), interrogative pronouns/adjectives (*who, whom, whose, what, which*).
  * **8.1.2 Anaphoric**: Personal pronouns, possessive adjectives/pronouns, demonstrative adjectives and pronouns, relative pronouns (*who, whose, whom, which, that*, omission), reflexive/emphatic pronouns (*myself, yourself*), reciprocal pronoun (*each other*), definite article (*the*), adverbs (*here, there, now, then, so*), propword (*one, ones*), substitute verb (*do*).
* **8.2 Indefinite**: Indefinite article (*an*), indefinite pronouns (*someone, somebody, no one, anybody, anyone, nobody, each, everybody, everyone, something, anything, nothing, everything, all, both, some, it, you*), indefinite determiners, adverbs (*somewhere, nowhere, anywhere, sometimes, never, always*), semi-deictics (*person, man, people, thing, do*).
